🤖 AI Lead Management & CRM Automation

An automated lead management system built with n8n to capture, process, organize, and follow up with potential customers automatically.

🚀 Overview

This workflow automates the lead management process from the moment a lead is received until the follow-up stage.

It connects CRM, notifications, email, and data management tools into one automated workflow, reducing manual work and improving lead response time.

⚙️ Workflow

      ↓

📌 How It Works

1. A new lead enters the workflow.
2. n8n processes and organizes the lead information.
3. The lead is added or updated in the CRM.
4. The relevant team receives a notification.
5. The workflow monitors the follow-up status.
6. If the lead is not followed up within the required time, an automatic reminder is triggered.
